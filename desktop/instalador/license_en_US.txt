HUROH Terms of Use and Data Protection

1. What HUROH is
HUROH is a personal app to organise finances, calendar and tasks. It is only for recording and organising: it is not a bank, it does not move money and it does not give financial advice.

2. Your account
To use HUROH you need a Google account: your HUROH account is created the first time you sign in with Google. HUROH does not create or store passwords; access security (password, two-step verification) is managed by Google and is your responsibility, as is the information you record. You must be at least 16 years old or have permission from a parent or guardian.

3. What data we keep
Account: the name, email and photo of your Google account (you can change the name and photo in the app). We never have access to your Google password, Gmail or contacts.
What you record: transactions, categories, events, tasks and app preferences.
We do not collect location, contacts, advertising data or usage statistics.

4. What we use the data for
Only to make the app work: to create and identify your account and to store and sync your information between your devices. The legal basis is the performance of this contract, that is, providing the service you asked for (GDPR, Article 6(1)(b); LGPD, Article 7, item V). We do not use the data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not build profiles and we do not make automated decisions about you.

5. Where it is stored
In the cloud: on Supabase, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Each account can only see its own data.
On your computer: a local copy so the app opens quickly and works offline, and a daily backup in Documents\HUROH\backups (the last 14 are kept). When you sign out, the local copy is deleted; the backups stay on your PC and only you control them.

6. Who we share it with
No one, except the technical services that are needed and that process the data only on our behalf: Supabase (hosting of accounts and data) and Google (to sign in to your account). Your data is only handed to authorities when the law requires it.

7. Security
Connections are encrypted (HTTPS) and access to the data is limited to your account. The security of your computer and password also depends on you. If there is a data breach that may affect you, we will tell you and the authorities within the legal deadlines.

8. How long we keep it
We keep the data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account: the account and all data are deleted immediately from our servers and from this device and, if you choose, also the backups on your computer. We do not keep copies. Technical copies held by the hosting service may remain and are deleted automatically within 30 days.

9. Your rights (GDPR and LGPD)
At any time and free of charge you can: find out what data we have about you, correct it, delete it (Settings → Delete account), take a copy (Settings → Export data), restrict or object to the processing and withdraw your consent.
